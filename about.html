Company overview

Who we are

Voltrix Electronics India Pvt. Ltd. distributes semiconductors and provides engineering and supply chain services to automotive, industrial, energy and consumer customers across India.

  • Founded 2008, headquartered in Noida
  • Offices in Pune, Bengaluru and Chennai
  • Wholly owned by a global trading group
  • Dedicated quality analysis centre
